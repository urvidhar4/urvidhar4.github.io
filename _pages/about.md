I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad (IIMA). My research interests lie in law and economics, development economics, and political economy, with a focus on judicial performance, the functioning of courts, and institutions in India. Before starting my PhD, I was a Research Associate at the Centre for Economic Data Analysis (CEDA), Ashoka University, and an Academic Associate at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore. I hold a master's degree in economics from the Gokhale Institute of Politics and Economics, Pune, and an undergraduate degree in mathematics from the University of Delhi.
