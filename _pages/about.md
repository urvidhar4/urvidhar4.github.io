I am a fourth-year PhD student in Economics at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad, advised by Tarun Jain. My research studies the performance of courts in India — how judges are selected, how they interact on the bench, and how institutions inherited from colonial rule shape judicial efficiency today. I also work on political economy, including the long-run effects of gender quotas in urban local government. And of course, I LOVE SANCHIT
